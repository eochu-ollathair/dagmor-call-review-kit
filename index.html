Four calls, four review questions

These conversations are fictional. Read each, decide what a reviewer should record, then compare your answer with the notes below.

1. Was a callback agreed?

Customer: Could you ring me tomorrow around lunch?
Agent: Yes, I'll sort that out.
Customer: Great, thanks.

Ask: Was a callback booked? What record would you check?

2. Who earned the praise?

Customer: Nine out of ten for the driver. He carried the boxes in when he saw my bad back. But accounts still haven't returned the money you billed twice.

Ask: Who earned the praise, and what remains wrong?

3. Did the required step happen?

Agent: Before we finish, I need to go through one more point—
Recording ends.

Ask: Can you mark that point as covered?

4. Was the objection handled well?

Customer: That sounds dearer than what I use now.
Agent: Fair point. Which part of your current bill are you comparing it with? I can explain the difference, and you can decide whether it is worth changing.
Customer: Send me the details. I haven't decided.

Ask: What did the agent do well, and what outcome can you record?

Answer notes

  1. The words show an offer and a general time, not a confirmed booking. Check the booking record or the rest of the original call.
  2. The driver earned the praise. Double billing and the missing refund remain open.
  3. The recording ends before the step is heard. Check whether the full original recording exists; do not guess a pass or a failure.
  4. The agent acknowledged the concern and asked a useful question. The customer requested details but did not agree to buy.

For a real review, keep the recording filename, the words supporting each finding, the chosen script and any human correction together.
